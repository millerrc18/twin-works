If no hard shims are needed, you may N/A the lines in the router.
SHIMMING GAP LIMITATIONS
If gap is less than 0.005 inch, no shimming is required
If the gap is not greater than 0.030 inch for machined parts, 0.060 inch for sheet metal parts, this process applies.
If a gap exceeds maximum gap, hand pressure may be applied. The maximum load that can be applied to close the gap is 20 lbs. This can be applied using a calibrated push/pull force gauge. Any other method of applying force that has been demonstrated not to exceed this limit may be used.
If after applying hand pressure, per above instructions, the gap exceeds the above criteria, MRB action will be required.
This shimming process may be used in conjunction with a maximum liquid shim thickness of 0.010" (see Process 5)

Solvent Clean:

a) Aluminum oxide paper - #320 A
b) Aluminum oxide abrasive pads, 280-400 grit per A-A-58054, Type I Grade A
c) Abrasive pads – fine or ultrafine – Scotch Brite/3M or standard abrasive
After abrading, using a clean lint-free cloth dampened with acetone and solvent wipe all surfaces of the shim.
Wipe dry with a clean dry lint-free cloth before the solvent evaporates.
Ensure the shim surfaces are free of oil, grease, fingerprints, and contaminants.
Allow the shim to air dry completely.
Apply Chromate Conversion Coating:
Remove cap. Hold Applicator device with applicator tip downwards.
Activate the device by pressing tip against a firm surface. New applicator device will charge in 30 to 45 seconds. When the conversion coating solution wets the top, release the pressure. Do not allow tip to cause puddling on the firm surface. Applicators previously in use may need only minimal tip-charging.
Apply the conversion coating to the aluminum surface in smooth, even strokes. Do not allow the material to puddle on surface.
A fifty (50) percent overlap of the strokes will give the required coating quality.
On trimmed edges apply the film with overlap stroke starting at the mid-point of the previous stroke.
Allow the first film coating to dry completely prior to second coating application.
NOTE: coating must not be rinsed with water or solvent after application.
After first coat has dried, apply a second coat (including to trimmed edges). On flat surfaces, apply at a 90 degrees cross-coat to the first coat application. Second coating must be applied within 5 minutes of the first coating application.
Allow the second coating to dry completely prior to painting/coating.
Painting/coating must be performed within the time limits stated in GAMPS 3116.

Assemblies with Faying Surface Sealant
(a) When a faying surface sealant is specified on the Engineering drawing, the fasteners should be
installed starting from the center of the part and working away from the center in all directions whenever possible.
NOTE: Installation from center to edge aids in extruding excess sealant to the part edges where it
can be removed.
(b) Faying surface sealed assemblies shall be fastened within the sealant application time limit per
the applicable sealant specification to ensure squeeze out of the excess sealant.
(c) Faying surface sealed assemblies that cannot be fastened within the sealant application time limit
shall be bolted together with temporary fasteners to squeeze out excess sealant. The maximum
distance between the temporary fasteners shall be five (5) inches.
NOTE: Spring type Clecos, by themselves, are not considered adequate to accomplish the
clamp-up required to squeeze out excess sealant.
Installation Tooling
The pulling gun should be held perpendicular to the material surface while pulling the fastener. Blind
bolt installation tooling should be operated per the manufacturer’s recommendations. Manual hand
operated blind bolt guns shall not be used for double action bolt installation (i.e., manual Huck gun).
NOTE: Double action blind bolts must not be installed with single action guns. Doing this will
result in improper seating of the locking ring and a structurally unacceptable installation.
Single Action Installation Tooling
Single Action blind bolts shall be installed with single action non-shifting installation tools. Single action
blind bolts with an optional drive washer (Figure 3) may be installed with an approved manual hand
operated blind bolt gun.
NOTE: The optional drive washer functions as integral anvil for driving during installation.
