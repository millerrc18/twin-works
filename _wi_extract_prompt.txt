There are certain operations in this document that must be performed in an “EMA”, Environmental Monitored Area”. 50 feet (15 meters) from prepreg or uncured adhesive that is not covered
Process That Disperse Fibers – Examples include: Minor Sanding, Cutting of dry fiber, Peel ply removal from cured parts

APPENDIX 4 – SEALANT SQUEEZE-OUT CLEANING PROCESS
CLEAN EXCESSES SQUEEZE-OUT PER GAMPS 7115:
Take a dry approved cleaning cloth, and wipe sealant off in one direction.
Lightly dampen an approved cleaning cloth with GAS115A1 solvent – DO NOT apply excess cleaning solvent.
Do not allow GAS115A1 solvent to wet the sealant which is to remain on the assembly.
Remove the selected sealant by wiping in one direction only – wipe from the clean area towards the contaminated area.
Follow the damp solvent wiping by wiping with a dry approved cloth to remove the solvent and any remaining sealant.
NOTE: Over-contamination of the solvent and cleaning cloth will cause spreading of solvated sealant over the assembly surfaces.
Repeat steps above as often as necessary using fresh areas of cleaning cloth lightly dampened with fresh solvent.
When all sealant and solvent have been completely removed by wet and dry wiping, complete the cleaning operation by wiping the remaining solvent residue from the surface using an approved cloth wetted with acetone.
